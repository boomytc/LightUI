---
title: Stop saying “make a modal”
date: 2026-08-24
summary: Floating is only the skin. Name whether it interrupts the current task, and whether it sticks to the trigger.
related: overlay-taxonomy
---

“Make a modal” describes something covering the page. What people actually hit is an interrupt model and an anchor that do not match.

Four avatar actions become a centered card, so the whole page is blocked. A delete confirm closes on the scrim, so a miss-tap undoes the confirm. A multi-field edit is stuffed into a small centered card, so it cannot scroll or sit next to the list. They all look like modals. They are not the same machine.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and interrupt / attach.

- **Modal**: must be handled first. A dangerous action must not close on the scrim.
- **Drawer**: a weak interrupt. Slides in from the right; the list stays visible.
- **Popover**: does not interrupt; stuck to the trigger. 2–7 items; more than that is a drawer.

The mix-up is always a centered modal. An avatar menu should stick to the trigger. Fields beside a list should slide in from the right.

A notice does not interrupt the current task, in [Notice](/s/notify-taxonomy). A panel that opens downward and commits one value is [Dropdown](/s/dropdown-taxonomy). How the questions chain is on the [map](/graph).
