# confirm-taxonomy study

Study of destructive action safeguards and the confirmation ladder.

- `study.json` declares the slug, asks, and links.
- `idea.md` explains the 7 levels of the confirmation ladder and why one-size-fits-all modals fail.
- `src/lib/machines.ts` holds pure algorithms (hold duration progress, swipe threshold calculation, type token match, selection checklist completeness).
- `src/StudyView.tsx` exports the interactive playground.
- `src/StageView.tsx` exports the stage fixture.
