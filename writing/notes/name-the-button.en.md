---
title: Stop saying “make a button”
date: 2026-08-24
summary: Being clickable is only the skin. Name the weight first: primary, secondary, or weak — one filled button per region.
related: button-taxonomy
---

“Make a button” describes the look: something you can press. What people actually hit is weight that does not match the job in this region.

Three filled buttons on a download card, so it is unclear which to hit first. Forgot password is filled too, and it competes with sign in. Cancel and confirm in a dialog weigh the same. They all look like buttons. They are not the same weight.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and how heavy this click is.

- **Solid**: filled; the one main action in this region.
- **Outline**: a stroke, no fill; does not compete.
- **Text**: no border, no fill; a quiet out.

The pair people mix up is Solid and Outline. One is the only press that should commit in this region. The other is the secondary that does not compete.
