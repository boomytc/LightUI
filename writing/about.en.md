# About

LightUI is a UI/UX notebook you can click into.

It keeps interactions I have implemented, and ones I have seen elsewhere that are worth trying again. Each **study** has a playground and one extracted rule. The **notes** say why it is worth keeping, instead of copying the algorithm a second time.

This is not a component library, and not a product shell. A new study starts with the idea, then the demo. Empty folders are not placeholders. Only the interaction itself lives here.

Source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/boomytc/LightUI).
