“Make a cooler scrollbar” describes a decoration on the right. What people actually hit is a job that does not match.

Still at the top, but already a column of position dots, so “there is more” is gone. Already moving, and the arrow still bobs. A click feels like a section, and lands on a scroll fraction. The pane fits, and the decoration stays. They all look like scrollbars. They are not the same machine.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and what it reports.

- **Native**: overflow belongs to the OS; the thumb is continuous.
- **Cue**: only at the top; a click walks one screen.
- **Track**: only with overflow; a click is i/(n−1), not a heading.

The pairs people mix up: a cue is not a track; dots are not section anchors. Do not stitch the first two into one morph.
