---
title: Stop saying “make a dropdown”
date: 2026-08-15
summary: Opening downward is only the skin. Name the commit rule first: one value, a set, a path, an action, or a span of days.
related: dropdown-taxonomy
---

“Make a dropdown” describes a panel that opens downward. What people actually hit is a commit rule that does not match the job.

Skills close after one pick, so the second tag never lands. A province is stored as the finished region. Publish hides the default action behind a chevron, so sending the piece takes an extra click. They all look like dropdowns. They are not the same machine.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and when the panel closes. Select closes on pick. Multi stays open; chips peel off the trigger. A grouped title files the list — it is not a path. A cascader parent only expands; a leaf commits. A split button’s body must fire. Mega is navigation. Dates are two ends, with the past locked.

The pair people mix up is Grouped and Cascader. One files a roster. The other walks a tree to a leaf.
