A membership card often gets a glow on the whole card, or a sheen that floods the face. Copy and price light up together, the edge stops being an edge, and the accent turns into fog.

The highlight is a short arc on the border. Stack a `conic-gradient` in a transparent border; register the angle with `@property` and spin it. A solid inner fill keeps the cone in the stroke. Use the brand accent only, not a rainbow. Animate only `--beam-angle`. A pause pins a corner; reduced motion leaves a static stroke.
