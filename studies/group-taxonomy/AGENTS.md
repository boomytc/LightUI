# group-taxonomy study

Study of semantic grouping, card reduction discipline, and spatial rhythm.

- `study.json` declares the slug, asks, and links.
- `idea.md` explains the 5+1 grouping techniques, why cards are abused, and why relationships determine grouping.
- `src/lib/machines.ts` holds pure data structures, quiz evaluation, pattern validation, and keyboard mapping.
- `src/StudyView.tsx` exports the interactive playground with instant comparison (`cards` vs `grouped`).
- `src/StageView.tsx` exports the stage fixture.
