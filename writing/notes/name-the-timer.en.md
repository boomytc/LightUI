“Make a timer” describes a number that moves. What people actually hit is the wrong direction.

Free study is drawn as a fake pomodoro with a 25-minute cap, pretending to finish. A focus block only grows, so remaining time is invisible. Hitting zero fires a toast, and the number goes negative. They all look like timers. The direction is not the same.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and whether this span counts up from 0 or down from N minutes.

- **Stopwatch**: no session cap; the number is how long has passed.
- **Focus**: counts down from N minutes; the number is how long is left; it stops itself at 0 — never negative, never a toast.

The pair people mix up is Stopwatch and Focus. Growing a countdown, or capping a stopwatch as a fake pomodoro, both break. Switching pages while it is running must still show this span in the top bar — that is a rule, not a third timer.
