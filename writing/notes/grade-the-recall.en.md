“Make flashcards” describes a prompt, an answer, tap to move on. What actually breaks is a commit that does not match the job.

Swipe to the next card with no grade, and tomorrow is the same pile. “Knew it” sits on the prompt face, so an interval is committed before the compare. Forgot and fuzzy both land on tomorrow. Nothing due today is written as “no data”. The three grades are built as cancel / OK. They all look like flashcards. They do not commit the same thing.

The flip is for comparison. Before the flip, only “Reveal answer”. What you submit is an interval, not a cut to the next frame.

- **Forgot**: the count resets; the card goes to the end of today’s pile. Not “tomorrow, same as fuzzy”.
- **Fuzzy**: the count stays; see it tomorrow.
- **Knew**: the count goes up; leave on 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days.

Nothing due today is not “no data”. This region should say why it is empty, and where to go next.
