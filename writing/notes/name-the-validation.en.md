---
title: Stop saying “add form validation”
date: 2026-08-24
summary: An error appearing is only the result. Name the timing first: on blur, in that column at once, or all at once on submit.
related: validation-taxonomy
---

“Add form validation” describes the result: a red line if the fill is wrong. What people actually hit is a speak-time that does not match the job.

The name is still being typed, and two characters already get scolded. A past day is picked, the month grid closes, and that column stays quiet until submit. Publish looks disabled and cannot be clicked, so the misses stay invisible. The inline miss floats away as a toast and no longer points at a field. Submit opens a confirm modal while the fields themselves stay clean. They all look like validation. They are not the same machine.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and when the error speaks.

- **On blur**: speaks when the cursor leaves this field.
- **Inline**: this column is given an illegal value; speak under the field as it closes.
- **On submit**: clicking publish marks every miss at once; a grey-looking button still receives the click.

The mix-up is using submit to cover all three timings. Typing is interrupted, a bad pick stays silent, or a dead grey button locks people out.

Typed or picked is not when the error speaks, in [Controls](/s/control-taxonomy). Inline errors are not a toast, in [Notice](/s/notify-taxonomy). How the questions chain is on the [map](/graph).
