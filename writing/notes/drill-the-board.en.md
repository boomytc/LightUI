title: Stop saying “make a dashboard”
date: 2026-08-24
summary: Numbers, cards, and a chart are only the skin. Name whether the board drills from the result, or serves KPI, chart, and table on one platter.
related: dashboard-layers
---

“Make a dashboard” describes the look: numbers, cards, a chart. What people actually hit is a flattened hierarchy.

Hunting why DAU dropped, KPI, trend, and detail are all there on open, so the row that moved the most is buried. Clicking a card only swaps the mark; the grain stays the same. Three stacked sections you scroll through look layered, but they were still served at once. They all look like dashboards. They are not the same machine.

Ask three things first: the name, the scene, and whether a click reveals the next layer.

- **Layered**: click a result, then the next layer. Open on KPIs only; a card opens the dimension; a row opens a short detail.
- **Platter**: KPI, chart, and table in view — scan, do not drill.

The pair people mix up is stacked sections and Layered. One is still a platter. The other waits for a click.
